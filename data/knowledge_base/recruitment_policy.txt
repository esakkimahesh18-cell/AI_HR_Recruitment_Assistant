RECRUITMENT ASSISTANT KNOWLEDGE BASE

1. Resume screening should be based on job-related qualifications and skills.
2. Protected characteristics must not be used for candidate scoring or ranking.
3. AI recommendations are advisory and must be reviewed by a human recruiter.
4. Interview questions should relate to the job description and candidate experience.
5. Missing skills should be presented as development gaps, not as personal judgments.
6. Candidate information should be handled securely and only for legitimate recruitment purposes.
7. A match score is an aid for prioritization and is not a final hiring decision.
